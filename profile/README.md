# PeakSky 🏔️

**Know when the sky will be clear.**

PeakSky is a small startup building a free, easy-to-read **Blue-Sky Index** (0-100) for any location. It combines smoke, haze and cloud forecasts into one number, so you can tell at a glance whether the sky will be clean and blue.

## Who it's for

- 📷 Photographers chasing clear, saturated skies
- 🥾 Hikers and outdoor lovers planning their days
- 🔥 Anyone watching wildfire-smoke and haze season

## What you get

- The index for any location: today, the next 24 hours and a 7-day outlook
- A daily ranking of the clearest cities in North America

PeakSky is free while in beta and has been live since June 2026.
